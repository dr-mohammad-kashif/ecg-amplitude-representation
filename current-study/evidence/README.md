# Evidence Record

**Status:** Active  
**Revision:** 0.1  
**Date:** 30 September 2026

The evidence record preserves the scientific material used to shape the active study.

The literature review will be developed as separate documents. This directory is intended to keep the underlying source record intact while those documents are assembled.

Sources are retained when they:

* establish scientific or methodological facts;
* close a candidate research question;
* support a study design decision;
* provide a reporting or reproducibility standard;
* establish current LLM access conditions;
* identify a relevant prior art system;
* reveal a failure mode that should be controlled;
* or materially change the interpretation of the research gap.

A paper that argues against the present direction is not discarded. Its role is recorded.

## Evidence classes

**Evidence fact**

A direct claim supported by a peer reviewed publication, formal standard, official dataset record, or current official provider documentation.

**Evidence supported inference**

A design conclusion that follows from more than one source and is not directly stated by one source.

**Investigator defined choice**

A controlled decision made because the study requires a specification that the literature does not uniquely determine.

**Computed result**

A value produced by execution, simulation, or another documented analysis.

## Citation rule

Scientific literature will use numbered Vancouver style where appropriate. Provider documentation will be cited separately in access records. Dataset citations will follow the dataset or publisher's recommended citation where available.

No reference is entered as complete until its bibliographic metadata have been checked against an authoritative source.
