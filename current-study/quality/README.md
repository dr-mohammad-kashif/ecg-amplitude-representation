# Research Quality Controls

**Status:** Active  
**Revision:** 0.1  
**Date:** 30 September 2026

The active study uses a separate quality control record for scientific content, citations, document structure, provenance, and presentation.

The purpose is ordinary research quality. The documents should read as scholarly working material, not as promotional material or as explanations of a drafting system.

Every active document will be checked for:

* scientific accuracy and status of claims;
* citation completeness and Vancouver consistency where applicable;
* consistency with the locked study decisions;
* clear distinction between evidence and investigator defined choices;
* reproducible file naming and internal links;
* conventional academic sectioning and typography;
* truthful version and date information;
* absence of fabricated results or retrospective rewriting;
* removal of unnecessary drafting commentary and reader directed process explanations;
* consistent terminology and abbreviations;
* simple conventional punctuation;
* clean tables and figures where used;
* preservation of provenance and research history.

A full checklist will be added before the first formal protocol document is frozen.
